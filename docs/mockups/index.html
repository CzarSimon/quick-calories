Quick Calories

Clickable design mockup. All figures are sample data built from the Q4 training schedule at 82 kg body weight.

Scenario: Tuesday 12:10. Breakfast is confirmed. The pre-lunch snack notification has fired but isn't confirmed yet. Monday closed 120 kcal over target, so Tue–Sun were each lowered by 20 kcal.

  • Tap Ate as planned on the notification, or tap any slot to adjust it.
  • Log dinner from the open budget card.
  • In Schedules, change kcal, protein or fat and carbs refill the rest. Set carbs by hand to see the imbalance prompt. Set a deficit and toggle the days it applies to.
